# No Chain-sourced media or supplier text

We display imagery we own or licence, at the Ingredient level, and never
Chain-hosted product photos. We likewise do not store or display
supplier-sourced product descriptions or nutrition text.

Prices a Chain sets for itself are *created* rather than *obtained*, which is
the strongest part of our position under the Database Directive's spin-off
reasoning. Photos and supplier-authored text have no such defence — product
photography is an ordinary copyright exposure needing no database theory at
all, and supplier data is squarely "obtained". Because the project intends to
ship commercially later, the cost of this decision is paid once now rather
than as a UI rebuild. Ingredient-level imagery is also far cheaper: Basket
Items name requirements, not Products (see ADR-0001), so roughly 200 images
cover the catalogue instead of tens of thousands.
