# Resolution Corrections are shared, not private

When a user corrects the Product a Resolution chose, the Correction is
reviewed and folded back into the shared Ingredient vocabulary — it does not
stay scoped to that user.

Own-brand equivalence has no identifier solution by construction and, as of
this writing, no public dataset or published method; every incumbent falls back
on fuzzy name matching. Accumulated human Corrections are therefore the one
asset in this project a competitor cannot acquire by scraping harder or buying
a feed, and they compound with use. Keeping Corrections private to each user
would discard that entirely. The consequences to accept: Corrections need
review before promotion (a bad one degrades every user), and sharing them is a
decision to surface in the privacy terms when accounts arrive.
